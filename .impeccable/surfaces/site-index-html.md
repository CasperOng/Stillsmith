# Stillsmith — site

Persona: Persuade. Audience: people who can use the app (iOS 17+, sideload via Feather) and people looking at the developer's work. Action: install, or leave understanding the mechanism.

## Direction contract

THESIS The page is lit by one projector beam in a dark booth: a still is a frame held in the light. It refuses the category arrangement of a gradient hero above equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD Matte black booth metal, port-glass haze, one warm beam (#FFF1D0 → #F2A03D) as the only strong field, warm paper for the contact sheet, brass hairlines, stamped mono labels. Syne 800 display, Space Grotesk body, JetBrains Mono for timecode only. Geometry is circular (reels, port glass, aperture) and ruled (film path).

STORY The visitor sees a frame held in a beam, drags the transport to a moment, watches a mark appear at that timecode, then reads the spec sheet and installs from Feather. The credit closes it.

FIRST VIEWPORT Full-bleed black. Left: wordmark at display scale, one sentence, two actions (Feather, GitHub), mono meta (iOS 17+, iPhone & iPad, unsigned). Right: a port-glass aperture lit by a skewed beam, holding the real home screenshot in a phone frame. Across the bottom, a ruled timecode rail with a playhead that follows the pointer and a MARK control. seed: 8d40d397f93c01475eb20846a5bd97c2

FORM Projection booth, position 6 of the grounded list. The register: the beam is the page's light, the contact sheet is the page's evidence, the transport is the signature interaction.

RAISES FROM DECLINED CHALLENGERS
- The single axis (deep-dive): one vertical film-path rule every section aligns to.
- The only red (VU bridge): red is reserved for destructive actions and nothing else.
- Ballistic motion (VU bridge): playhead and frame tabs carry mass, not linear tweens.

FINISH unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
