      What it keeps

      Everything the app does is below. There is no account, no subscription and no server.

      Full resolution
      Every frame is written at the video’s own resolution. Nothing is scaled down or re-encoded.
      Frame-accurate
      Marks are taken at the playhead’s exact time, with zero tolerance either side.
      Cancelable
      Stop a long extraction at any point. The frames already written stay saved.
      Album control
      Save into a custom album, created the first time it is needed. Without full photo access, frames go to the library instead.
      Every entrance
      Videos arrive from the photo picker, the share sheet, Files or AirDrop. When a build cannot take a shared video, the extension says so and offers Files.
      Accessible by default
      Dynamic Type at every size, VoiceOver labels and hints, 44 pt hit targets, and Reduce Motion honoured.
      Local only
      No network calls, no analytics, no accounts. Frames go from the video to your photo library.

      Install it your way.

      Stillsmith is unsigned software. Any tool that can sign an IPA will run it — on the device, or from a Mac.

      On the device

      Add the source in your signing app. It is an AltStore-style feed, so Feather, AltStore, SideStore, ESign and LiveContainer all read it. Copy it for the app you use:

      https://stillsmith.is-my.app/sources

      Sideloadly, or any IPA installer

      Download the newest unsigned build and let the tool sign it from your Mac or PC.

      https://github.com/CasperOng/Stillsmith/releases/download/latest/Stillsmith-unsigned.ipa

      Xcode

      Clone the repository, open Stillsmith.xcodeproj, and run it on your device. The build steps are in the readme.

      github.com/CasperOng/Stillsmith
